# Leqad official visual identity — October 23, 2026

The user's chosen **baseline logo** is the circular engineered emblem and heavy geometric **Leqad** wordmark. The center of the symbol is **Hay's Russet `#793327`**, not bright orange.

- Primary dark navy: **`#0E2943`**.
- Light version: navy lockup on ivory/white (`leqad-official-horizontal-light-v1.svg`).
- Dark version: ivory lockup on navy (`leqad-official-horizontal-dark-v1.svg`).
- Symbol-only derivatives for favicon, compact places, and program icons (`leqad-official-symbol-*-v1.svg`).
- The vector-shaped paths are **digitally traced from the approved October 2026 raster** and are not represented as the original artist-authored vector master.

Always preserve the exact symbol silhouette and heavy wordmark proportions. Do not reconstruct the wordmark using a similar typeface. No black rectangular backgrounds, framing, CSS blend effects, filter recoloring, rasterized SVG embedding or oversized transparent margins.

**Important color distinction:** Existing metallic CTA rust `#B74B21` is a UI treatment, not the official **logo** russet. Do not automatically recolor the entire website.

**Historical assets** `leqad-approved.png`, `leqad-navbar.png`, and `leqad-tab-v1.svg` remain in Git solely for rollback; they are not the active identity and must not be copied into new product surfaces.

The social sharing images are historical screenshots and should be recaptured after the new logo ships; do not represent those as an up-to-date logo master.
