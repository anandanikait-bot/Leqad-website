## Changes delivered in this pass

- Removed Partners Cpu and School icons, preserving numbers, headings and functional controls.
- Removed the redundant "Collaboration" eyebrow from the About closing invitation.
- Removed outer demo and CAD canvas shadows. Reduced regular and completed teaching-panel shadows from `shadow-md` to `shadow-sm`.
- Removed `blur-md` and `blur-none` from the hero transition. Rotation, the 400 ms opacity/translation transition, reduced-motion handling, stable heading name and reserved phrase height remain.

Numbered learning rows, text-based product-status columns, the collapsed Partners planner and static About preview already existed before this follow-up. They are baseline facts, not new changes.

## Typography comparison and decision

The comparison used all three phrases at 375, 768 and 1440 pixels. See the [desktop comparison](screenshots/visual-followup/typography/comparison-1440.png), [tablet comparison](screenshots/visual-followup/typography/comparison-768.png), [mobile comparison](screenshots/visual-followup/typography/comparison-375.png) and [raw record](screenshots/visual-followup/typography/comparison.json).

Retain Instrument Serif. Its narrower italic text keeps the changing phrase distinct from the fixed Space Grotesk heading. The Space Grotesk alternative is readable, but repeats the heading's heavier texture and reduces that distinction. This is a visual judgment about this page, not a universal endorsement of serif accents or familiar font pairings.

The CAD artwork, logo assets, halftone footer, color values, metallic CTA and current font files remain intact. The checklist prompted a review of their purpose; it did not justify replacing the site's identity or removing useful CAD controls.
